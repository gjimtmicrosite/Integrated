Participant & Workshop Details

How this quiz works

  • 10 questions are picked randomly from a bank of 60 Functional English questions.
  • Questions cover grammar, vocabulary, sentence correction, communication, workplace English, and comprehension.
  • 60-second timer per question—auto-moves if time runs out.
  • Scores 50%+ get a downloadable Certificate of Completion.
This test measures English accuracy, vocabulary, grammar, and practical communication skills.

What this quiz checks

Tests practical Functional English skills aligned with the SkillX communication and professional-English module.

Grammar Basics Vocabulary Sentence Correction Everyday English Workplace English Communication Comprehension Professional Usage

Outcome Bands & Next Steps

  • 9-10 correct: Excellent—Strong Functional English. Ready for advanced professional communication.
  • 7-8 correct: Good—Good practical English. Refine accuracy, vocabulary, and workplace usage.
  • 5-6 correct: Fair—Practice Needed. Focus on grammar, vocabulary, and sentence formation.
  • 0-4 correct: Support Recommended—Strengthen Functional English foundations.
