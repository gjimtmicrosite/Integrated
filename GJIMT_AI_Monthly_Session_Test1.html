Participant Details

How this test works

  • 15 fixed multiple-choice questions based only on the first AI Monthly Session.
  • Topics include AI basics, AI chatbots, Generative AI, prompts, ChatGPT and responsible use of AI.
  • 60 seconds are provided for each question.
  • If time runs out, the test automatically moves to the next question.
  • 50% or above is required to receive the AI Monthly Session certificate.

This assessment is designed for Session 1 of the GJIMT AI Monthly Sessions.

What this test checks

The assessment checks basic understanding of Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, AI assistants, prompting, ChatGPT, student use cases and responsible evaluation of AI-generated information.
